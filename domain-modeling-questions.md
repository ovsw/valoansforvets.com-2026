## Historical measurement baseline

Source: [VA Loans Lead Source Audit](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1S-xAsBj38vsNqkYHdYtSioRNNSk2mMOW/edit?usp=sharing), covering January 2025 through August 2026.

- 915 captured lead submissions over 20 months: 649 mortgage-folder submissions and 266 ScoreApp submissions, an average of 45.8 submissions per month.
- 446 submissions were captured from January through August 2026, an average of 55.8 per month.
- YouTube was attributed to 542 submissions, or 59.2%. This includes all 266 ScoreApp submissions, which the audit assigns to YouTube by rule rather than from a lead-stated source.
- Jimmy Vercellino funded 46 loans totaling $23,449,938 from January 1 through August 17, 2026.
- Ten funded loans match a captured lead by full name: 21.7% of the 46 funded loans. Four more are possible last-name-only matches. The remaining 32 funded loans, or 69.6%, do not match either captured-lead folder.
- The audit's 1.09% tracked lead-to-close figure is a lower bound, not a reliable funnel conversion rate. It compares 20 months of submissions with eight months of funded loans, does not deduplicate every source into unique people, and cannot see leads that entered through calls, texts, direct applications, the CRM, past-client activity, or other untracked routes.
- Historical bookings, held Consultations, booking-to-held rate, and stage conversion rates were not measured. These values remain unknown rather than estimated.
